Step 1

Learn It in 3 Seconds: 4 Swipes

The whole game runs on these four moves. Get the muscle memory down before you worry about strategy.

⬆️

Swipe Up · Jump

Clears barriers and hops onto train roofs. Jump a half-beat early so you don't land right on a car's edge.

⬇️

Swipe Down · Roll

Ducks under low signs and tunnel mouths. Rolling has recovery lag, so don't chain it between two obstacles.

⬅️

Swipe Left · Lane Left

One of three lanes. When a train's coming from a side track, switch a beat early instead of at the last second.

➡️

Swipe Right · Lane Right

Same idea, mirrored. Train your eyes to look ahead instead of at your feet — it buys you an extra reaction beat.

Step 2

Dodging Guide: 3 Obstacle Types

90% of crashes come from these three situations. Learn the pattern and you'll react before you even think.

🚆 Oncoming / Side Trains

See a headlight flash or hear the horn — decide instantly whether to jump on top or switch lanes. If two adjacent tracks are both blocked, dodge toward the open lane instead of forcing a jump.

🚧 Barriers & Poles

Low barriers: just jump. High barriers: a hoverboard lets you smash straight through without slowing down. Without one, don't freeze right in front of it — decide a screen ahead.

🚦 Low Signs & Signals

Roll under them. The roll's invincibility window is short, so don't rely on it to tank a chain of obstacles — you'll still need to switch lanes.

Step 3

Scoring Tips: Where the Points Actually Come From

The gap between casual players and high scorers almost always comes down to this system.

  • Distance runBase score climbs automatically the longer you survive — don't slow down just to weave for coins
  • Coin streaksIndividual coins are worth little, but a magnet can fill an entire lane in seconds
  • Hoverboard ridingFaster movement plus one free hit — score climbs quicker while it's active
  • Mission bonusesFinishing a run's mission (like "jump 20 times") gives a one-time boost — grab it along the way, don't detour

Step 4

Power-Up Cheat Sheet

Picking one up isn't the same as using it well — timing matters more than quantity.

🧲
Collector

Magnet

Auto-pulls nearby coins. Save it for a coin-dense straightaway — popping it on an empty lane is wasted value.

🎒
Flight

Jetpack

Brief flight plus invincibility — your lifesaver through the densest obstacle clusters. Save it for the toughest stretch.

🛹
Shield

Hoverboard

Absorbs one hit and speeds you up. Worth summoning manually at the start of a run for a built-in safety net.

✨
Multiplier

2x Coins

Timed doubling — pairs best with a magnet. Prioritize this one when you're grinding coins for a daily challenge.
